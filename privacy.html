Privacy policy

The short version

This site is a pre-launch brochure. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. There are no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or marketing cookies on these pages.

Forms

The form on our homepage does not send anything anywhere yet. Anything you type stays in your browser and is discarded when you leave the page. When booking opens, this policy will be updated before any form begins transmitting data, and it will say exactly what is collected and why.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and writes nothing to your browser's local storage.

Third parties

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting font files from Google's servers. No other third-party resources are loaded.

Changes

If our practices change at launch, the updated policy will be posted here with a new date before the change takes effect.
